Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Nerd HQ Publisher is a private tool that posts videos only to the owner's own Nerd HQ accounts.

What it collects: only the sign-in tokens and basic profile info (such as account name) that a platform returns when the owner connects their own account. It does not collect data from viewers or any other users.

How it is used: solely to upload and publish videos to the owner's account. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Storage and deletion: tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from the platform's account settings, which deletes access.
