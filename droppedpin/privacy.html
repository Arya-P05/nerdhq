Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Dropped Pin Planet Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Dropped Pin Planet YouTube channel (youtube.com/@droppedpinplanet) to upload and manage videos on that channel and read its analytics.

Google user data it accesses: via the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, with the scopes youtube, youtube.upload, and yt-analytics.readonly, it accesses the channel's basic info, its videos and playlists (to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists, and schedules), and the channel's own analytics reports. It does not access data from viewers or any other users.

How it is used: only to publish and manage videos on the owner's own channel and to review the channel's performance. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion: OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer and never published. Access can be revoked any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which ends access; locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Dropped Pin Planet Publisher uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
