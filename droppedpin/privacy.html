Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Dropped Pin Planet Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Dropped Pin Planet YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@droppedpinplanet) to upload and manage videos on that channel and read its analytics.

Google user data accessed: via the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API (scopes youtube, youtube.upload, yt-analytics.readonly), the channel's basic info, its videos and playlists (to upload videos and set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and schedules), and the channel's own analytics reports. No data from viewers or any other users.

How it is used: only to publish and manage videos on the owner's own channel and review its performance. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion: OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer and never published. Access can be revoked any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Dropped Pin Planet Publisher uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
